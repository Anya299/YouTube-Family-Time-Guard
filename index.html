Family Time Guard

A simple reminder to balance screen time with family time.

⏱️ Screen Time Helps monitor YouTube watching time.
🔔 Reminder Shows a reminder when the configured limit is reached.
❤️ Family Time Encourages taking a break and spending time together.

How to Install

  1. Download the Family Time Guard extension files.
  2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions/.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked.
  5. Select the extension folder.
  6. Open YouTube and use the extension.

Family Time Guard is a personal screen-time reminder project.
